--- layout: single author_profile: true title: "About Me" ---

I am a researcher and systems engineer with over 15 years of experience in large-scale distributed computing. I am a core contributor to DeepSpeed, an open-source framework for distributed deep learning, and serve on its Technical Steering Committee. My work spans compiler optimizations for distributed training, sequence parallelism for long contexts, and inference serving.

As a principal researcher at Microsoft, I helped train foundation models on clusters with 2,000+ GPUs. My work there included Phi-3 and MAI-DS-R1. Earlier, at Japan’s National Institute of Information and Communications Technology (NICT), I developed middleware for large-scale data analysis.

After Microsoft, I led development of a Ray-based framework for hybrid parallelism at Anyscale.
